Why AI-generated content needs guardrails

Modern content teams face new challenges when AI enters the workflow. Here's what happens without proper safety checks.

Hallucinations

AI invents facts, statistics, and citations that don't exist. Without verification, false information spreads through your content.

Content Drift

Every prompt generates different content. Your message changes uncontrollably with each regeneration, losing consistency.

Compliance Risk

GDPR and DSGVO require verified, auditable content. Untracked AI-generated text creates legal liabilities and compliance failures.

Brand Inconsistency

Different AI outputs across pages dilute your brand voice. Your website becomes a patchwork of conflicting tones and styles.
